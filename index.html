The Biggest Boot Loader

Firmware Updater For The Biggest Foot

  1. Plug The Biggest Foot in to Eurorack power via ribbon cable
  2. Plug a USB-C cable in to The Biggest Boot sub-board, and a desktop computer running Chrome.
  3. Click the Connect button.
  4. Select the "USB to UART Bridge Controller". If you don't see this option listed, try a different USB-C cable.
  5. Choose to erase & flash.

5.0.0 (July 26, 2025)

This update focused mainly on bug fixes that affected production units.

  • Changed: Hold down SHIFT while powering on to connect your balance board for the first time.
  • Changed: Press the front button to connect your balance board after pairing at least once.
  • Fixed: Balance board will automatically reconnect if interrupted.
  • Added: Version number of firmware displays on boot.
  • Fixed: One-shot playback jumped to ranom position at the end.
  • Pressing Record after pressing Trigger would not record.
  • Fixed: Calibration could be completed on low battery causing a crash.
  • Fixed: Calibration would change user weight even if not completed.
  • Changed: Lefty orbits around the center at boot.
  • Changed: Pressing trig randomizes the orbit center just like CV triggers.
  • Known Issue: There is now no way to bring Lefty back to center.
